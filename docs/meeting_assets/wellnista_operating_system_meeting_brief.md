# Wellnista Operating System Meeting Brief

## Meeting Purpose

Help the 3-person Wellnista team move from vision and overlapping work into a clear operating system:

```text
Vision -> Roles -> OKRs/KPIs -> Delegation -> Tracking -> Weekly Action
```

## Current Situation

### Facts

- Wellnista is currently running two units with one small team:
  - Wellnista Tech: Wellnista AI and WellBook marketplace.
  - Wellnista Event: Walk & Learn Thai and B2B events.
- The current named team is Alice, P'Tao, and Sim.
- The main operating problems are unclear ownership and a vision that is not yet fully converted into measurable execution.
- The Foundation Playbook identifies Alice as the main bottleneck risk.
- The user wants to operate as Business Development for Wellnista.

### Inferences

- The company does not primarily need more ideas right now; it needs a decision and execution machine.
- Business Development can help the company become more systematic by turning market activity into evidence.
- The best meeting output is not discussion only; it is a signed-off prototype of roles, scorecards, delegation rules, and one tracker.

### Unknowns

- Whether Alice, P'Tao, and Sim already accept the proposed role split.
- Whether the June 30 launch target is still current.
- Which tracker tool the team prefers: Google Sheet, Notion, Trello, ClickUp, or another tool.
- Who will be the final DRI for the operating tracker.

## Meeting Outcomes

By the end of the meeting, the team should agree on:

1. Current company situation.
2. Role split for Alice, P'Tao, and Sim.
3. Company OKR/KPI system.
4. Delegation flow from CEO to role owners.
5. Tracking system and weekly review ritual.
6. BD role as the market-evidence engine.

## Recommended Meeting Flow

| Time | Topic | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-10 min | Situation framing | Shared view of current problems |
| 10-35 min | Role split | Draft role canvas for 3 people |
| 35-60 min | OKR/KPI system | 3 company objectives and owner metrics |
| 60-80 min | CEO delegation system | Delegation protocol and decision rights |
| 80-105 min | Tracking system | Board fields, stages, and DRI |
| 105-120 min | Commitment | First 7-day action list |

## Recommended Tone For The Meeting

Frame the meeting as:

```text
This is not about controlling people.
This is about making work visible, reducing founder bottleneck, and helping every person know what they own.
```

Avoid framing it as:

```text
We need bureaucracy.
We need everyone to report to me.
We need a heavy corporate system.
```

## Business Development Positioning

The BD role should say:

```text
My role is to help Wellnista turn external conversations into evidence:
which partners matter, what pain is real, what people will try, and what should become product or event strategy.
```
