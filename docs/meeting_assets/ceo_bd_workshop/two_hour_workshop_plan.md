# 2-Hour CEO + BD Workshop Plan

## Workshop Objective

Create a lightweight operating agreement between CEO and Business Development so Wellnista can close its startup gaps and turn vision into weekly execution.

## Pre-Work

### CEO prepares

- Top 3 company priorities for the next 30 days.
- Biggest current bottleneck.
- Which decisions CEO wants to keep.
- Which decisions CEO is willing to delegate.
- 5 warm-intro opportunities or strategic partner names, if any.

### BD prepares

- Top 20 lead/partner candidates.
- 3 proposed ICP segments.
- 3 strongest pitch angles.
- Proposed weekly BD scorecard.
- What support is needed from CEO.

## Rules For The Workshop

- No vague agreement. Every decision must become owner, metric, deadline, or next action.
- One owner per outcome.
- Debate for 10 minutes max, then the accountable owner decides.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Do not create a heavy system. Build the smallest system that will be used next week.

## Agenda

| Time | Section | What To Do | Output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0-10 min | Frame the current reality | Agree what Wellnista is facing now: two units, small team, unclear ownership, CEO bottleneck, weak tracking | Shared problem statement |
| 10-25 min | Name the startup gaps | Use the gap canvas: ownership, priorities, delegation, tracking, BD mandate, evidence, finance/legal/ops gaps | Gap list ranked by urgency |
| 25-45 min | Close ownership gaps | Decide who owns each critical outcome; use DRI/RACI thinking | Owner map v1 |
| 45-60 min | Define CEO-to-BD delegation | Agree what BD can decide alone, what needs CEO approval, and what CEO must provide | CEO+BD decision-rights agreement |
| 60-80 min | Build the 30-day BD sprint | Pick ICP, weekly targets, lead volume, outreach rule, discovery target, pilot target | 30-day BD sprint plan |
| 80-95 min | Create tracking system | Choose tracker fields, weekly check-in rhythm, and scorecard | Tracker v1 spec |
| 95-110 min | Risk and support map | Name what can block BD: missing contacts, unclear offer, no warm intros, product readiness, CEO bottleneck | Blocker list + owner |
| 110-120 min | Commit | Read decisions aloud; each owner confirms next action and deadline | 7-day action list |

## What To Say At The Start

```text
The goal today is not to make the company more corporate.
The goal is to make execution clearer.
By the end, every important gap should have an owner, a next action, and a way to track progress.
```

## The 6 Gaps To Close

| Gap | Symptom | Workshop Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership gap | Everyone helps, no one owns | Assign one DRI per outcome |
| Priority gap | Too many ideas compete | Pick top 3 company priorities |
| Delegation gap | CEO gives direction but no clear owner/metric | Use Delegation Card |
| Tracking gap | Work lives in chats or memory | One tracker and weekly review |
| BD mandate gap | BD helps generally but does not own clear outputs | Define BD charter and scorecard |
| Evidence gap | Decisions are opinion-led | BD pipeline produces market evidence |

## Decisions That Must Be Made

1. What are the CEO's top 3 priorities for the next 30 days?
2. What does BD own?
3. What can BD decide without CEO approval?
4. What must CEO approve?
5. Which partner segment comes first?
6. What is the 30-day BD scorecard?
7. Where will work be tracked?
8. What is the first 7-day action list?
