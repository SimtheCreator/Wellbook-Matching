# CEO + BD Agreement

This is the working agreement between CEO and Business Development.

## Purpose

Business Development exists to turn external conversations into evidence, opportunities, pilots, and partner commitments.

```text
BD is not only sales.
BD is the market-evidence engine for Wellnista.
```

## BD Owns

| Area | Ownership |
|---|---|
| ICP clarity | Define and refine which partner segments matter now |
| Lead pipeline | Source, verify, score, and prioritize leads |
| Outreach | Draft and manually send outreach or prepare CEO-approved messages |
| Discovery | Learn partner pain, current process, urgency, objections, and commitment signal |
| Pilot design | Convert qualified interest into a small test |
| Evidence | Report what the market is saying back to CEO/Product/Ops |
| Scorecard | Weekly BD numbers and next actions |

## CEO Owns

| Area | Ownership |
|---|---|
| Strategic priority | Decide which business objective matters most now |
| High-value relationships | Handle or support key relationships when founder credibility matters |
| Brand boundary | Approve sensitive messaging and positioning |
| Final tradeoffs | Decide when BD, Tech, and Event priorities conflict |
| Warm intros | Provide introductions where CEO has leverage |

## BD Can Decide Alone

- Which leads to research first within the agreed ICP.
- Which outreach angle to test first.
- Which leads to park because fit is weak.
- Which discovery questions to use.
- Which insights are worth bringing to weekly review.

## BD Needs CEO Approval For

- Public partnership announcements.
- Paid commercial terms or revenue-share commitments.
- High-risk brand claims.
- Legal, PDPA, payment, or data-sensitive commitments.
- Partnerships that require major product or event resources.

## Support BD Needs From CEO

| Need | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Top 3 company priorities | BD needs to know what kind of opportunity matters |
| Warm intros | Speeds up high-value partner conversations |
| Positioning boundaries | Prevents BD from promising the wrong thing |
| Product/event readiness | BD needs to know what can actually be offered |
| Weekly 30-minute review | Keeps learning connected to company decisions |

## Weekly BD Scorecard

| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Leads reviewed | 20 |
| Leads manually verified | 10 |
| High-fit leads prioritized | 5 |
| Outreach messages sent manually | 10 |
| Replies | 3 |
| Discovery calls | 2 |
| Pilot proposals | 1 |
| Partner commitments or strong next steps | 1 |

## Weekly Review Questions

1. Which segment responded best?
2. Which pain repeated more than once?
3. Which pitch angle worked?
4. Which objections appeared?
5. What should Product or Event learn?
6. Which CEO support is needed next week?
7. What should BD stop doing?
