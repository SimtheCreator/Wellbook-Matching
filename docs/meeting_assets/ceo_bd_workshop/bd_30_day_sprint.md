# BD 30-Day Sprint

## Sprint Objective

Prove which wellness partner segment is most worth pursuing now and create at least one concrete pilot or partner commitment.

## Segment Priority

Pick one primary segment for the first 30 days.

| Segment | Priority | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Free/community wellness organizers | Recommended first | Lower friction, good for RSVP/attendee/QR/community growth pain |
| Paid studios/practitioners | Second | Stronger monetization, but may need clearer booking/payment offer |
| Event partners | Parallel if CEO has warm intros | Useful for revenue and event-to-app conversion |
| Corporate wellness | Later unless strategic | Higher effort and longer sales cycle |

## 30-Day Targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---:|
| Leads reviewed | 80 |
| Leads manually verified | 40 |
| High-fit leads prioritized | 20 |
| Outreach messages sent manually | 40 |
| Replies | 12 |
| Discovery calls | 8 |
| Pilot proposals | 4 |
| Partner commitments / strong next steps | 2 |

## Weekly Plan

| Week | Focus | Outputs |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Setup and first outreach | ICP confirmed, tracker live, 20 leads scored, 10 messages sent |
| 2 | Discovery and pattern finding | 4 calls, top pains summarized, pitch refined |
| 3 | Pilot design | 2-3 pilot concepts proposed, CEO support used for warm intros |
| 4 | Commitment and decision | 1-2 commitments or strong next steps, segment recommendation for next month |

## Lead Qualification Rules

Score 1-5:

| Criteria | Ask |
|---|---|
| ICP fit | Is this a Thai wellness/community/movement organizer? |
| Admin pain | Do they seem to manage RSVP, attendance, payment, or DMs manually? |
| Recurrence | Do they run recurring events/classes? |
| Access | Is there a verified human contact path? |
| Commitment potential | Could they join a small pilot? |
| Strategic value | Does this support Tech launch, Event revenue, or event-to-app conversion? |

Decision:

- 24-30: pursue now.
- 18-23: research or contact if easy.
- 12-17: park.
- Below 12: do not pursue now.

## Pilot Options

| Pilot | Best For | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|
| RSVP + attendee list | Free/community organizer | Organizer agrees to use one list for next event |
| QR check-in | Recurring event/community | % attendees checked in or joined waitlist/app |
| Partner listing | Paid studio/practitioner | Partner agrees to list 1-3 activities |
| Booking/payment test | Paid provider | One paid booking or clear payment workflow test |
| Co-hosted event | Event partner | Attendance, revenue, and event-to-app signups |

## End-Of-Sprint Decision

At the end of 30 days, decide:

1. Which segment should BD focus on next month?
2. Which pitch angle worked best?
3. Which product/event offer should be built or simplified?
4. Which leads should be advanced, parked, or dropped?
5. What support does CEO need to provide next?
