## Suggested Early Roles

| Role | Purpose | Owns | Metrics | Does Not Own |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business & Strategy Lead | Keep the business direction and 90-day objective clear | Positioning, prioritization, decision log | Objective progress, key decisions made | Every task and every customer conversation |
| Customer & Partnership Lead | Find and learn from organizers, partners, and early customers | Interviews, outreach, lead pipeline, follow-up | Interviews, meetings, interested leads, pilots | Product decisions alone, finance/admin |
| Product & Service Lead | Turn validated pain into a simple offer, MVP, or service workflow | Offer design, prototype, manual booking test | MVP shipped, feedback collected, conversion signal | Outreach volume alone |
| Marketing & Community Lead | Package message and demand generation | Content, community positioning, message tests | Content shipped, replies, signups | Finance, internal operations |
| Operations Lead | Make work visible and keep deadlines moving | Task board, weekly review, blockers, process docs | Tasks completed on time, blockers resolved | Main sales ownership |
| Finance & Admin Lead | Keep money, documents, and budget disciplined | Budget, basic admin, pricing assumptions | Budget clarity, payment/admin readiness | Creative direction or product scope |
