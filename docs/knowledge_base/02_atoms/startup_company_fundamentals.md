# Startup Company Fundamentals Atoms

## Atom: Start With Role Map

Tags: #startup #company-setup #roles  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

A startup should begin company setup by mapping people to strengths, weaknesses, energy, business need, and metrics. Role clarity should come before formal titles or an org chart.

## Atom: Use Lead Roles Before C-Level Titles

Tags: #startup #roles #team-design  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

Early teams should prefer output-based "Lead" titles such as Customer & Partnership Lead, Product & Service Lead, Operations Lead, and Finance & Admin Lead. A title is useful only when it states owned output, decision authority, reporting metric, and what the role does not own.

## Atom: Convert Ideas Into Objectives, Projects, Tasks, Owners, And Scorecards

Tags: #startup #execution-system #okr  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

Ideas become executable when they are translated into hypotheses, 30- to 90-day objectives, projects, tasks, owners, and scorecards. Each task needs an owner, due date, output, and status.

## Atom: Validate Before Building Big Product

Tags: #startup #validation #lean-startup  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

Do not build a large app, website, or automation system before there is evidence that the customer pain is real, interest is real, and users or partners are willing to pay or commit. Start with interviews, mockups, manual service, and small selling tests.

## Atom: Weekly Review Creates Accountability

Tags: #startup #operating-rhythm #accountability  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

A weekly 30-minute review should cover scorecard, done, not done, blockers, decisions, and next actions. A meeting is only useful if it ends with decisions, owners, and due dates.

## Atom: One Owner Per Task

Tags: #startup #operations #execution  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

Small teams often say "everyone helps," but execution improves when each task has one clear owner. Work without an owner, deadline, and output is not operationally real.

## Atom: Seven-Day Startup Setup Sequence

Tags: #startup #first-week #operating-system  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\startup_operating_system_guide.md`  
Status: active

The first seven days should produce a role map, one-page vision, 90-day OKR, core project list, task board, simple MVP or prototype, and first weekly review. This is enough structure to start learning without overbuilding.
