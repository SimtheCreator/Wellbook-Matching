# Wellnista Business Units And Team Atoms

## Atom: Wellnista Currently Has Four Business Lines

Tags: #wellnista #business-units #strategy #focus  
Source: User clarification, 2026-06-17  
Status: active

The user clarified that Wellnista should currently be understood as four related business/product lines:

1. Wellnista AI: a companion and assistant for women who want to reach their dreams, set or manifest goals, convert them into daily activities, and use checklist-style support.
2. Wellbook Platform: a marketplace/operating platform for wellness events where organizers can list activities, customers can book and pay, and organizers can manage attendees. Sim currently looks after this unit.
3. Nubsuk: a separate food-photo calorie estimation app with a strong Thai-food advantage. It may later be merged into Wellnista AI, but is not currently the main focus.
4. Events and Wellness Workshops: CEO Ice's strength and preferred work, useful for community, trust, and revenue, but also a source of focus loss if not connected to a business loop.

## Atom: Current Strategic Focus Should Be One Core Loop

Tags: #wellnista #strategy #focus #wellbook #wellnista-ai  
Source: User clarification and synthesis, 2026-06-17  
Status: active

Because the company has only three people and four business lines, the team should not operate as if it has four full companies. The recommended near-term core loop is:

Ice's brand and events create trust and demand -> Wellbook captures booking, attendance, partner supply, and market evidence -> Wellnista AI later recommends the right activities from goals/manifestations -> Nubsuk later becomes a nutrition/food insight module when there is enough AI user traction.

For the next 30-90 days, Wellbook plus CEO-led events/workshops should be the commercial validation engine. Wellnista AI should remain a narrow prototype or guidance layer. Nubsuk should be parked as a future module unless it directly supports the active Wellnista AI user loop.

## Atom: Wellbook Killer Feature Should Be Goal-To-Activity Matching Plus Organizer Operating Kit

Tags: #wellbook #product #features #marketplace #wellness  
Source: User request and synthesis, 2026-06-17  
Status: active

Wellbook should avoid becoming a generic event marketplace. Its most defensible killer feature direction is "goal-to-activity matching for users plus a simple organizer operating kit for wellness events."

Organizer-side MVP features should prioritize event landing pages, RSVP/booking/capacity, payment or payment instructions, attendee list/status, QR check-in, LINE-friendly reminders/message copy, waitlist/cancellation handling, post-event feedback, and a basic organizer dashboard.

User-side MVP features should prioritize browsing by goal rather than category, beginner-friendly tags, trust signals, checklist/calendar save, post-event reflection, and later AI recommendations once enough supply and behavior data exist.

## Atom: Wellnista AI Should Integrate Slowly As Demand And Guidance Layer

Tags: #wellnista-ai #integration #wellbook #nubsuk #product-strategy  
Source: User request and synthesis, 2026-06-17  
Status: active

Wellnista AI should plug into Wellbook and eventually Nubsuk, but only in stages. The strategic fit is strong if the integration helps women reach dreams through clearer daily actions and relevant real-world activities. The risk is overbuilding a super app before user demand, event supply, and retention are validated.

Recommended staged integration:

1. Phase 1: Wellnista AI prototype maps goal/manifestation to daily checklist and manually recommended events.
2. Phase 2: Wellbook events appear as recommendation cards inside the AI flow.
3. Phase 3: Booking, attendance, and reflection data personalize the next action.
4. Phase 4: Nubsuk becomes a nutrition module only after Wellnista AI has active users and a clear need for food tracking.

## Atom: Wellnista Meeting Tone Should Be Friend-Team Clear, Not Corporate-Heavy

Tags: #wellnista #team-culture #meeting #startup-ops  
Source: User clarification, 2026-06-17  
Status: active

The user clarified that the current team is three friends, so meeting materials should not sound too corporate or punitive. The tone should be clear enough to reduce confusion but soft enough to preserve trust and friendship.

Recommended language: use "owner, output, deadline, next action, weekly scorecard" rather than heavy corporate terms like formal KPI, OKR, RACI, accountability, or delegation process. The meeting should be framed as helping everyone get less tired and making it easier to help Ice, not as evaluating people or creating hierarchy.
