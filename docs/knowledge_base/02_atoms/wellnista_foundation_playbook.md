## Atom: Wellnista Currently Has Two Units And One Team

Tags: #wellnista #company-structure #operating-model  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

The Foundation Playbook frames Wellnista as two units run by the same 3-person team: Wellnista Tech and Wellnista Event. Tech includes Wellnista AI and the Wellbook marketplace, while Event includes Walk & Learn Thai and B2B events.

## Atom: The Two Immediate Problems Are Ownership And Action Plan

Tags: #wellnista #execution #roles #okr  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

The playbook names two operating problems: unclear ownership and a strong vision without a measurable plan. The recommended fix is a combined role and OKR system, where roles answer "who owns what" and OKRs answer "what winning this quarter means."

## Atom: Proposed Three-Person Role Architecture

Tags: #wellnista #team-design #roles  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

The playbook proposes Alice as CEO + GM of Wellnista Event, P'Tao as CTO of Wellnista Tech, and Sim as COO across both units. The split gives Alice vision, brand, event revenue, and tie-breaks; P'Tao product and technical delivery; and Sim operations, growth, supply, analytics, support, and event logistics.

## Atom: RACI Requires One Accountable Owner Per Workstream

Tags: #wellnista #raci #accountability  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

The playbook recommends a RACI matrix with exactly one Accountable owner per workstream. This is meant to prevent shared accountability from becoming no accountability, especially across Tech, Event, and company-wide work.

## Atom: Alice Bottleneck Is The Main Operating Risk

Tags: #wellnista #founder-risk #operations  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

The playbook identifies Alice as the main bottleneck because she is proposed to own vision, events, brand/content, product direction, pricing, finance, fundraising, and tie-breaks. Recommended mitigations are to move event logistics and content distribution to Sim, give P'Tao autonomy over technical delivery, and consider an Events / Community coordinator as the first hire.

## Atom: Events Must Feed The App

Tags: #wellnista #events #growth #app-conversion  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

Events are not only a standalone revenue line; they are also a room full of target users. The playbook recommends a QR sign-up at every event and a tracked event-to-app conversion metric owned operationally by Sim.

## Atom: Current Workshop Should Produce Roles, RACI, OKRs, And Cadence

Tags: #wellnista #workshop #operating-rhythm  
Source: `C:\Users\LENOVO\Downloads\Wellnista_Foundation_Playbook.pdf`  
Status: active

The recommended workshop is a half-day session or two 2-hour blocks. It should end with role canvases, signed-off RACI, three company OKRs, personal OKRs, a shared tracker, weekly cadence, and a decision protocol.
