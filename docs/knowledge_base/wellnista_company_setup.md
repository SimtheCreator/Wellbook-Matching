# Wellnista Company Setup

## Purpose

Create a lightweight company operating structure for Wellnista / WellBook so the team can turn ideas into validated partner acquisition work.

## What To Do First

Start with a Role Map before titles, product buildout, automation, or a full company org chart.

The first working session should answer:

1. Who is on the team?
2. What is each person's strength?
3. What drains or weakens each person?
4. Which business output should each person own?
5. What metric proves that ownership is working?

Use `templates/role_map.md` for this.

## Facts

- Wellnista / WellBook is focused on B2B partner acquisition for Thailand wellness organizers.
- Current user clarification on 2026-06-17 frames Wellnista as four related business/product lines: Wellnista AI, Wellbook Platform, Nubsuk, and Events / Wellness Workshops.
- Wellnista AI is intended as a companion and assistant for women who want to reach their dreams, set or manifest goals, convert them into daily activities, and use checklist support.
- Wellbook Platform is the current unit Sim looks after; it helps wellness organizers list activities, customers book/pay, and organizers manage attendees.
- Nubsuk is a separate food-photo calorie estimation app with a Thai-food strength and may later become a Wellnista AI module.
- Events / Wellness Workshops are CEO Ice's strength and should be used as a community, demand, and proof engine rather than an unmanaged distraction.
- Current outreach emphasis is smaller free or community organizers first, then paid booking prospects.
- Free or small community groups should be pitched around discovery, RSVP, attendee lists, QR check-in, and community growth.
- Paid studios or practitioners should be pitched around booking, payment, capacity management, reduced LINE or DM admin, and launch offers.
- Missing LINE, email, phone, or IG details should be treated as unknown until manually verified.
- The June 2026 Foundation Playbook frames Wellnista as two units run by one 3-person team: Wellnista Tech and Wellnista Event.
- The named current team in the playbook is Alice, P'Tao, and Sim.
- Current user clarification names the CEO as Ice. Some earlier materials may refer to Alice; use Ice in current meeting materials unless the user says otherwise.
- The playbook identifies two immediate problems: unclear ownership and vision without a measurable action plan.
- The playbook proposes Alice as CEO + GM Events, P'Tao as CTO for Tech, and Sim as COO across both units.
- The playbook names Alice as the main bottleneck risk because she carries vision, events, brand/content, product direction, pricing, finance, fundraising, and tie-breaks.

## Inferences

- The first 90-day company objective should focus on proving partner demand, not building a large product system.
- The smallest useful operating system is: Role Map, 90-day objective, 3 core projects, task board, weekly review.
- Initial role names should use "Lead" instead of C-level titles to reduce ego and keep ownership tied to output.
- Given the playbook's current situation, Wellnista should run a role-and-OKR workshop before adding more tools, features, or outreach automation.
- Sim should likely be the tracker DRI because the playbook positions Sim as the operations backbone.
- Event-to-app conversion should be treated as a strategic bridge metric, not an afterthought.

## Draft 90-Day Objective

Prove that Thai wellness community organizers and small paid wellness providers have enough demand for WellBook to join a pilot or start a paid booking workflow.

## Weekly Review Cadence

Run one 30-minute weekly review:

| Agenda | Question |
|---|---|
| Scorecard | Which numbers improved or worsened? |
| Done | What shipped this week? |
| Not Done | What slipped, and why? |
| Blockers | What is stuck? |
| Decisions | What needs a decision now? |
| Next Actions | Who owns what by when? |
